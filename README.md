# 🍎 MacroSnap

MacroSnap is an AI-powered nutrition tracking app that helps users analyze food, track meals, and monitor their daily nutrition goals.

## ✨ Features

- 🤖 AI-powered food image analysis
- 🔥 Calorie estimation
- 💪 Protein, carbohydrate, and fat tracking
- 🍽️ Meal tracking and history
- 🎯 Personalized nutrition targets
- 📊 Weekly and monthly nutrition insights
- 📥 Export meal data

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- Python
- Streamlit
- Google Gemini AI
- SQLite
- Pandas
- OpenPyXL

## 🚀 How It Works

1. Create your profile and set your nutrition goal.
2. Upload a photo of your food.
3. Gemini AI analyzes the food and estimates its nutritional values.
4. Review or edit the results.
5. Save the meal and track your progress through the dashboard.
